"Giardino" — Italian for garden. A place tended with intention, where growth takes time and care.

Our Approach

Careful pruning, deeper growth

Deeper generosity is cultivated, not forced — with the same care and attention a gardener gives what's ready to grow. Three practices guide every engagement.

C

Clarity

We start by a careful assessment of where your fundraising program is — an honest, clear-eyed read on your donors, your team, and where the real opportunity for growth lives, offered with care, not judgement.

H

Honoured Relationships

Donors, staff, and board are invited into this work as partners — generosity always deepens fastest in relationships that are built on trust.

S

Stewardship

Like careful pruning, our work makes space for what's ready to flourish — leaving your fundraising, and the people who carry it, stronger long after we've stepped back.

What We Do

Three ways we work with you

Strategy

Transformational Giving

We help you see clearly who your highest-capacity relationships really are, then build a thoughtful, disciplined path to deepen them — so transformational gifts grow out of trust, not transactions. For organizations working internationally, that includes turning field impact into a donor narrative that lands, and building major-gift relationships with donors who may never set foot in the countries where the work happens.

Learn more →
Leadership

Fractional Leadership

Steady, senior fundraising leadership embedded in your team — for the season between hires, the push to scale, or the moment you need an experienced hand at the helm without adding a full-time role. Having led fundraising from inside international NGOs myself, I know the specific tension between field teams and HQ, and how to lead a program that has to work across both.

Learn more →
Facilitation

Team Retreats

One to two days, fully facilitated, to help your team pause, reconnect, and realign — restoring momentum and a shared sense of purpose in the work ahead.

Who We Serve

Wherever your fundraising is today

Small & growing shops

Raising under roughly $2M annually, ready to move from grassroots fundraising toward a real major gifts program.

Mid-size orgs scaling major gifts

An established program ready to professionalize moves management and grow gift size, retention, and donor loyalty.

High-revenue organizations

Sophisticated shops needing dedicated strategy for high-net-worth and ultra-high-net-worth donor relationships.

Teams needing realignment

Navigating restructuring, a leadership gap, or misalignment — needing steady, senior fundraising leadership to find footing again.

International development & global-facing organizations

INGOs, humanitarian and global health organizations, and faith-based missions working across borders — where donor relationships, field impact, and HQ strategy all have to align.

The Engagement

How an engagement unfolds

Discover

We begin by listening — spending real time with your team and senior leaders to understand your program, your donors, and where growth is possible.

Design

A collaborative, relational plan built together with your team — aligned with your organization's size and goals, never a generic playbook.

Deliver

Hands-on execution alongside your team, whether that's strategy, interim leadership, or facilitation.

Sustain

Systems and documentation handed off so progress holds long after the engagement ends.

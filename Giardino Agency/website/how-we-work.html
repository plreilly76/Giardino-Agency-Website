How We Work

Rooted in clarity, not complexity

Every engagement starts the same way — by understanding your organization before prescribing anything. Here's what that looks like, start to finish.

Discover

We begin by listening and learning — spending time with your donor pipeline, past campaigns, team, and board to understand where your fundraising truly stands. Usually 1–2 weeks.

Design

A collaborative, relational plan built together with your team — focused priorities, timeline, and the specific service mix that fits, never a generic playbook.

Deliver

Hands-on work alongside your team: strategy sessions, embedded leadership, donor management, or a facilitated retreat.

Sustain

Documentation, training, and a handoff plan so what has been built will keep running after we step back.

In Depth

Three ways we work with you

01 — Strategy

Transformational Giving

Major donor strategy built around your highest-capacity relationships — moves management, gift planning, and ask strategy designed to deepen trust before deepening the ask, backed by the case for support and donor-facing materials that make the ask land.

Ideal forOrgs ready to deepen major gift strategy
Typical length3–6 months
FormatRemote + on-site
  • Donor portfolio audit and segmentation by capacity and affinity
  • Moves management system design and rollout
  • Case for support development and major-gift proposal writing
  • HNW and UHNW donor communications and stewardship assets
  • Board and staff coaching on donor conversations
  • 90-day and annual major gifts plan with clear metrics
02 — Leadership

Fractional Leadership

Interim or embedded senior fundraising leadership — serving as your Director of Development, Chief Development Officer, or Advancement Director — for organizations between hires, scaling a program, or needing experienced direction without adding a full-time role.

Ideal forTeams in transition or growth
Typical length3–12 months
FormatEmbedded support
  • Acting as (or alongside) your Director of Development, Chief Development Officer, or Advancement Director
  • Team management, hiring support, and performance coaching
  • Budget, forecasting, and board reporting on fundraising KPIs
  • Continuity of major donor relationships during transitions
  • Structured handoff plan to a permanent hire
03 — Facilitation

Team Retreats

A two-day, in-person retreat built to do more than the name implies — this is about building a flourishing culture, real capacity, and shared strength, with honest realignment around mission and values. Fundraising teams are carrying acute pressure right now; this is the space to address it directly, then leave with the focus and direction that unlocks deeper generosity from donors.

Ideal forTeams needing reset or alignment
Typical length2 days
FormatIn-person
  • Pre-retreat interviews with your team and senior leaders to surface real dynamics, gaps, and opportunities
  • Two full days, in person, addressing strategy, team gaps, and realignment around mission and values
  • Facilitated sessions built for teams carrying real, acute pressure — not generic team-building exercises
  • Genuine team connection time woven throughout, plus concrete building blocks to leave with
  • Optional overnight extension with a guest speaker on a topic identified with your senior staff
  • A written summary with concrete next steps, plus an optional 30-day follow-up

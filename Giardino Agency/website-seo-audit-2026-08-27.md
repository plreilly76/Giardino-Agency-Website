# Giardino Agency Website — SEO & Discoverability Audit
_Site: https://www.giardinoagency.io (Squarespace, not yet public) — Audited 2026-08-27_

## Site as it stands
- Nav: Home, How we work, About, Contact
- Services: Transformational Giving (major donor strategy), Fractional Leadership (interim/embedded senior fundraising leadership), Team Retreats (1–2 day facilitated sessions)
- Positioning: boutique philanthropic consulting, Vancouver BC, "garden" (giardìno) brand concept — clarity, honored relationships, stewardship
- About page has a strong founder story (Philip Reilly — Scotland-born, international NGO background across Nepal, Malawi, Zambia, Ethiopia, Tanzania, Uganda, Rwanda, Kenya, Philippines)

## Gaps found

**Trust signals**
- No testimonials or client quotes
- No case studies or results (even qualitative)
- No client logos or sector examples
- No credentials/certifications (e.g. CFRE) or media mentions

**Content for discoverability**
- No blog/resources/insights section — biggest SEO gap. Competitors (Aperio Philanthropy, MM&C Consulting, Veritus Group) all publish ongoing content targeting search terms like "major donor strategy," "fractional development director," "nonprofit team burnout"
- No lead magnet (guide/checklist/framework) to capture emails — Aperio uses a newsletter signup
- CTAs are vague ("Learn More") rather than action-oriented ("Book a call," "Schedule a consultation")

**Structural / on-page SEO**
- Page titles look good already (e.g. "How We Work — Giardino Agency") — confirm every page has a unique title + meta description via Squarespace Page Settings → SEO tab
- No visible structured data (LocalBusiness / ProfessionalService schema) — matters for local/"near me" search as a Vancouver-based practice
- No FAQ section — easy win for FAQ schema and matches how prospects actually search ("how does fractional fundraising leadership work")

**Team/authority depth**
- Only one bio (founder) — fine for now, but add more if associates/facilitators join
- No downloadable one-pager; "who we serve" is only 3 situational bullets, not segmented by org size/budget

## What competitors do well (borrow these)
- **Aperio Philanthropy**: newsletter capture + homepage client testimonial + a visual "roadmap" that demystifies the engagement process
- **Veritus Group / MM&C Consulting**: recurring blog content targeting long-tail fundraising search terms — the main driver of organic discoverability over time
- Several competitors segment "who we serve" by org size/budget band, not just situation — helps prospects self-qualify and improves search-intent match

## Priority order before going public
1. Fill in Squarespace SEO settings (titles, meta descriptions, image alt text) page by page
2. Set up Google Business Profile (important for a Vancouver-based consultancy)
3. Add 2–3 testimonials or anonymized outcome stories
4. Add an FAQ block on the How We Work page
5. Plan a lightweight content cadence (even 1 post/month) — compounds over time for discoverability
